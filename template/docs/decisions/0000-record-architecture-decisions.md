# 0000: Record architecture decisions

**Status:** Accepted

**Context:** Choices made early (tools, standards, departures from them) are
hard to understand later without the reasons behind them.

**Decision:** Keep short decision records in `docs/decisions/NNNN-title.md`,
following Michael Nygard's format: Status, Context, Decision, Consequence.

**Consequence:** Each deliberate departure from a published standard gets a
record. Superseded records stay, with their status changed.
